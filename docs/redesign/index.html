    The person's photo of Tanacetum corymbosum is a stand-in from Wikimedia Commons; that species is not in the book. Other "your photo" thumbnails reuse catalog photos. Search in this prototype covers the 93 plants of the demo key plus all families and top genera.

    Offline sizes are the pictures only. Photos, the plate, and the range map average 0.66 MB a species, so 1,433 plants is about 950 MB. A plant in two regions is stored once: Middle Europe plus the Northeast is about 70 MB more, not another 570 MB. Antarctic is left out. No species lives only there. The regions are the floristic ones already on each plant.
